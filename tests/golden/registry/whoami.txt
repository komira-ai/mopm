usage: mopm whoami [--registry <prod|gamma|beta|url>] [--config-dir <dir>] [--json]

Show the account the token belongs to and its namespaces.

options:
  --registry <prod|gamma|beta|url>
      the registry (default prod)
  --config-dir <dir>
      where 'mopm login' keeps credentials.json (default ~/.config/mopm)
  --json
      print the registry's answer as JSON instead of text

Sends GET /api/v2/me (getMe) with the token 'mopm login' stored, or $MOPM_TOKEN.
