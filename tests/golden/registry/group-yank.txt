usage: mopm group yank <group_id> [--reason <reason>] [--registry <prod|gamma|beta|url>] [--config-dir <dir>] [--json]

Yank every version a publish group published.

The body is that of `setYanked`. `yanked: true` yanks the group's versions that are `published`; `yanked: false` restores
exactly the versions that yank changed. Allowed for an account that can write the namespace of every package in it now, whether or not it created the
group (a token outlives a membership change; an account that has lost the right is refused with `not_namespace_member`), with a token
that has the `yank` or `publish` scope for every member (read-only mode refuses it).

Only a group that is `visible` can be yanked or unyanked; a group that has not committed, or whose files are still being
released after the commit, is refused with `group_not_settled`, as is a yank while an unyank of the group is running and the
other way round. Asynchronous: the request records the intent, answers 202 and returns the group; the versions are yanked
(or restored) by a job, then repodata and the sparse index follow, and the group's `yank.state` reaches `yanked` (or
`restored`) when they are written. A version somebody yanked by hand before the group's yank stays yanked, and is not
restored by the group's unyank. The same request again while the yank or unyank runs answers 202 as it is; a group already as
asked (yanked and `yanked: true`, or never yanked or restored and `yanked: false`) answers 200 as it is.

arguments:
  <group_id>
options:
  --reason <reason>
      Required when `yanked` is true. Shown to people who locked the version.
  --registry <prod|gamma|beta|url>
      the registry (default prod)
  --config-dir <dir>
      where 'mopm login' keeps credentials.json (default ~/.config/mopm)
  --json
      print the registry's answer as JSON instead of text

Sends PUT /api/v2/groups/{group_id}/yank (yankGroup) with the token 'mopm login' stored, or $MOPM_TOKEN.
