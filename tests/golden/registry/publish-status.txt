usage: mopm publish status <publish_id> [--registry <prod|gamma|beta|url>] [--config-dir <dir>] [--json]

Show how a publish is going.

`pending`, `validating` and `indexing` are in progress; `published` and
`rejected` are final. A rejected publish burns the version number.
A publish that passed can still carry `warnings`.

arguments:
  <publish_id>
options:
  --registry <prod|gamma|beta|url>
      the registry (default prod)
  --config-dir <dir>
      where 'mopm login' keeps credentials.json (default ~/.config/mopm)
  --json
      print the registry's answer as JSON instead of text

Sends GET /api/v2/publishes/{publish_id} (getPublishStatus) with the token 'mopm login' stored, or $MOPM_TOKEN.
