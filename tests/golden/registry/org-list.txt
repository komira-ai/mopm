usage: mopm org list <namespace> [--registry <prod|gamma|beta|url>] [--config-dir <dir>] [--json]

List the members of a namespace and their roles.

Readable by an owner or a member of the namespace; any other account is `not_namespace_member`. A user
namespace has no members (`invalid_request`). Sorted by `user`.

arguments:
  <namespace>
      The namespace of the package, a GitHub login in lower case.
options:
  --registry <prod|gamma|beta|url>
      the registry (default prod)
  --config-dir <dir>
      where 'mopm login' keeps credentials.json (default ~/.config/mopm)
  --json
      print the registry's answer as JSON instead of text

Sends GET /api/v2/namespaces/{namespace}/members (listMembers) with the token 'mopm login' stored, or $MOPM_TOKEN.
